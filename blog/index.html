About Me

I’m a physics engine engineer at Bilibili, working on real-time simulation for games. My work centers on cloth simulation and parallel computing in Unreal Engine, with an emphasis on detailed physical behavior and efficient runtime performance.

I hold a master’s degree from East China Normal University, with a research background in physics-based simulation of different materials and GPU parallel computing. My research interests also include learning-based physical simulation and robotics simulation.

My long-term goal is to bring fast, physically realistic simulation to real-world applications beyond games.
